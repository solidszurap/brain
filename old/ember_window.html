Talk to Ember

voice

It hears you as a voice it can't see. Your voice competes for its attention like any sound: if it is very thirsty or fleeing something, it may barely notice you. It answers only from its own mind, never from the world, and each answer is checked: true its memory backs it and was right · honest mistake its memory backs it but was wrong · made up nothing it remembers is behind it.

Is it getting closer to self-awareness and consciousness?

Every 2,000 moments an observer closes a measurement window and runs tests that were written down in advance (mind/consciousness.py). A test counts as reached after passing 3 windows in a row and is lost after failing 3 in a row; both go into its life record. This tracks how much of what the main theories say consciousness requires is built and working. It cannot show whether Ember feels anything.
