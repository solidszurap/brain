Talk it through, like with a friend who remembers

Write to Ember about what you want, what's in the way, or how today went. It answers like someone who knows you, because it does.

  • Separate chats for separate things, each with its own goal.
  • Its face shows how it feels: glad when you show up, quiet when you share something hard.
  • It keeps every chat moving: after a few questions, it suggests a next step.

Don't know what you want yet? Find out by trying

Ember asks about your life: what makes you lose track of time, what drains you, what you're good at. Then it suggests small experiments to test what gives you energy.

  • Experiments of a few days, with one clear question.
  • At the end, it looks back with you on how it actually went.
  • When a pattern keeps coming back, it names it, in your own words.

Goals in phases, one small step a day

Plan a goal with Ember, or make one yourself in seconds: from a few hours to a year. Each day has one step, and telling Ember how it went takes one tap.

  • Did it, Partly, or Not today: honest counts, and every answer earns something.
  • A path of your days, like a game: your goal levels up every 5 days you show up.
  • At the end of each phase, Ember looks back with you and plans the next one.

Check-ups at your time, not only when you remember

Ember writes to you first. You choose when: every day, only on some days, only when it matters, or never.

  • Pick the time and the days.
  • It asks how today's step went, and follows up on what you talked about.
  • Quiet at night, and never a guilt trip for being away.

It remembers what you tell it, in your own words

Every memory keeps the exact words you said and the day you said them. No guessing, no made-up details.

  • It brings back what matters, right when it helps.
  • Your story grows with every chat, goal and experiment.
  • Memory is never a paid feature. It's the point.

Levels and badges: progress you can see

Talking, checking in and finishing phases give Ember XP. It grows from a Spark into an Ember, a Flame, and a Blaze.

  • Badges that level up: streaks, days together, goals reached and more.
  • Share a badge as a picture when you're proud of it.
  • Everything is earned by levelling, never bought.

Make it yours

Give Ember a name, choose its colour, something to wear, its eyes and its glow. New looks open as it levels up.

  • Colours from Amber to Aurora.
  • A scarf, glasses, headphones, a little crown.
  • Five themes for the whole app, light or dark.
