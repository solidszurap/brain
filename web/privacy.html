Privacy

Plain words, no tricks. Last updated 3 November 2026.

What Ember keeps

  • Your Google login's email and an account id (to know it's you).
  • Everything you and Ember say to each other.
  • What Ember remembers about you, each with the words you said it came from.
  • Your experiments, work sessions and how they went.
  • How many times Ember thought each day (to keep costs in check).

Where it's kept

In a database at Supabase and on a server at Render, both in Frankfurt, Germany (EU).

Who else sees it

To think, Ember sends what it remembers about you and your recent conversation to Anthropic's Claude API (its mind). Anthropic doesn't use API data to train its models by default; this processing may happen in the United States. Nobody else gets your data. It is never sold, never shared for advertising, and Ember shows no ads.

Your control

  • See and correct everything Ember remembers: "What it knows".
  • Download all your data as a file, any time: the menu (⋯) → Download my data.
  • Delete everything, your Ember and your account, any time: the menu (⋯) → Delete my Ember. It's gone for good, within seconds.

What Ember is not

Ember is not a therapist, a doctor or a crisis service. It never claims to have feelings. If you're having a hard time or thinking about hurting yourself, please reach out to people who can help right now: in Hungary call 116-123 (free, day and night), or 112 in an emergency; elsewhere, your local emergency number.

Age

Ember is for people aged 16 and over.
