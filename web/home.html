How Ember helps you find your purpose

Not by handing you an answer. By finding it with you, from your own life.

  1. First it gets to know you

    Honestly, I don't know what I want to do with my life.

    That's more common than it feels. When did you last lose track of time?

    Sketching. Fixing up my friend's flyer last week, I didn't notice two hours go by.

  2. Then a small experiment

    For 7 days, design one small thing a day, 20 minutes. Each evening, tell me if it gave you energy or drained you.

  3. After a few a direction

    The same thing keeps coming back, in your words: "I love making something people actually use." Shall we turn that into a goal?

Then, getting there, one day at a time

A goal becomes phases and one small step a day. You tell Ember how it went with one tap, and every day you show up lights up your path.

  1. Monday you talk it through

    I want to start running, but I always quit after a week.

    Then let's start gentle. Six weeks, in three phases. Today: walk 5 minutes, then run 1 and walk 2, six times.

    Your goal: Run 5 km without stopping, in 6 weeks

  2. Wednesday, 18:00 it checks up on you

    Today's step: same again, a little slower if it hurts. How did it go?

    Did itPartlyNot today

    Ten minutes in the rain still counts. Same plan tomorrow? +6 XP

  3. Two weeks later it looks back with you

    First phase done: twelve runs in two weeks, and you said going slow made it fun for once. Next phase: longer runs.

Questions people ask

Is Ember an AI?

Yes. Ember thinks with an AI language model. Everything around it (what it remembers, your goals, the check-ups, how it grows) is built so it knows your story and stays on your side. It's honest about what it is: ask it, and it tells you.

Who can read what I tell it?

Nobody reads your conversations, and they're stored in the EU. Privacy, in plain words.

Do I need to download an app?

No. Ember runs in your browser on your phone or computer. On a phone you can add it to your home screen, and it opens like an app and can send you notifications.
