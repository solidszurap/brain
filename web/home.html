A week with Ember

Here's how it goes when you tell it you want to start running.

  1. Monday you talk it through

    I want to start running, but I always quit after a week.

    What usually makes you stop?

    I go too hard, it hurts, and then I skip a day and that's it.

    Then let's start gentle. Six weeks, in three phases. This week: walk and run for 20 minutes, three times.

    Your goal: Run 5 km without stopping, in 6 weeks

  2. Wednesday, 18:00 it checks up on you

    Today's step: walk and run for 20 minutes. How did it go?

    Did itPartlyNot today

    Only 10 minutes. It started raining.

    Ten minutes in the rain still counts. Same plan on Friday? +6 XP

  3. Sunday it looks back with you

    First phase done. You went out three times, and you said going slow made it fun for once. Next phase: 25 minutes, with 2-minute runs.

Made for when you're stuck

When you don't know what you want. Ember suggests small experiments based on what you've told it, so you find out by trying, not by thinking in circles.

When you know, but don't get there. A goal becomes phases and one step a day. Ember checks in at the time you choose, and changes the plan with you when life gets in the way.

When you want someone who remembers. Every memory keeps the exact words you said and the day you said them. You can read, correct or delete all of it.

When a little reward helps. Ember grows as you make progress. Earn levels and choose how it looks.
