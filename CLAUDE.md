# Ember / brain

**Ember** ("Your companion who checks up on you and grows with you.", Szilard's tagline, 2026-11-06, "checks up on you" added the same day: on the login screen, the manifest and the meta description) is a small being with a face, now a web app (phone-first, Google login, one Ember per person). It helps you get what you want: if you don't know yet what to work towards, it helps you find out (experiments); when you know what you want, it helps you get there (goals). It works alongside you, and over time it becomes someone who knows your story. Szilard is its first user; he has no project yet, which is exactly the problem it's for.

**The goal** (Szilard, 2026-11-06): reach as many people as we can, and make it a business: a free plan with a daily limit plus Ember Plus via Stripe; no ads, no selling data. The space is proven but crowded (Finch: a pet that grows with your self-care; Tolan: an AI companion, $1M+/month; AI coaches with proactive check-ins). Ember's own angle: one companion that helps you *find* what you want (experiments) and then get there (goals in phases, daily checks, check-ups), with a memory that keeps your exact words. Judge new work by whether it helps more people start, stay and pay. **The big picture** (Szilard, 2026-11-07): the biggest help Ember can be is helping people find their purpose, then live it a step at a time; it's how Ember explains itself (`SYSTEM`: found together from their life and experiments, never handed to them), the front page's headline ("Find your purpose. Then live it, one step at a time." and a "How Ember helps you find your purpose" sequence), the descriptions, and the angle for advertising. README.md is the overview; DEPLOY.md is how it goes online.

Owner: Szilard (Hungarian, Budapest/Szeged; prefers direct, concrete, plain answers; gets lost in jargon; runs on Windows; not technical about hosting, so he relies on us for that). GitHub: solidszurap/brain.

## Run

```bash
pip install -r requirements.txt       # fastapi, uvicorn, anthropic, cryptography
python ember.py                       # local mode: your Ember in ember_state/, no login; http://localhost:8765
python ember.py --port 8799 --state <scratch dir>   # a separate Ember for trying things (never test on the real one)
python check_ember.py                 # Ember's self: about a second, no LLM
python check_server.py                # the server: a few seconds; fake mind, fake logins, fake Supabase
python -m server                      # online mode (Render); needs the env vars in render.yaml
```

## Map

- `server/app.py`, `create_app(store, auth, cfg, call, clock, ...)`:
  - `Users` caches one `Ember` per person and saves lines first, then the self. An Ember nobody has used for `IDLE_FOR` (an hour) leaves memory (`evict`, run after each check-up pass: saved first, kept if it's thinking or was used meanwhile); background looks (`get(touch=False)`: check-ups, the restart re-queue) don't keep one in memory.
  - `/state?v=1` is presence: it calls `arrive`/`update` lazily, since there is no global loop.
  - `MindWorker` has `mind_threads` queues and threads (`EMBER_MIND_THREADS`, default 4, 2026-11-06); a person always goes to the same one (`queue_of`, crc32 of the id), so one person's thoughts stay in order while different people are answered at once. Token usage is per thread (`llm.take_usage`).
    - Retries: the SDK's own, plus one more after `retry_delay`.
    - If the mind still fails: the `UNREACHABLE` line once, then a retry every `retry_every`.
    - A non-retryable error gives the `FAILED` line.
    - The daily cap gives the `CAPPED` line.
    - Each answer's time is logged (`mind answered <id> (<event>) in Ns`, a warning over 20 s). Claude calls time out after 30 s with 2 SDK retries (2026-11-06, after a goal check was answered 2 minutes late).
    - After a restart, unanswered last lines are re-queued.
  - The POST guard: `X-Ember` header and same host. `/stripe/webhook` is exempt: it checks Stripe's signature instead. Plus `/me/export` and `/me/delete`; delete cancels an active subscription first.

## Rules (decided; keep them)

1. **Never destroy a life.**
   - Never reset or overwrite `ember_state/`, and never touch live data to try things.
   - Use a scratch Ember (`--state`) or the checks.
   - Old lives move to `ember_state/archive/`.
   - Online deletion happens only through the user's own "Delete my Ember". Memories are deleted only by Forget.
2. **Memory is perfect for everyone, forever.** It's the core promise and never a paid feature. Keep costs flat by what goes into the prompt, never by forgetting.
3. **Not an imitation animal.** No food, hunger, sleep, seasons, or survival world. No simulated time while away: real time passes and it knows how much.
4. **Honest, lightly.**
   - Memories keep the user's exact words and the date. A memory without a matching quote is dropped.
   - Feelings (Szilard, 2026-11-04): Ember is warm and talks naturally ("I'm glad you went", "I missed hearing from you"), and the app doesn't point out what it lacks. Only if you ask it directly whether it really feels things does it answer honestly: software, wants that are real numbers, and it doesn't know whether that is feeling. It never lies about what it is. Its mood shows on its face and in its words (2026-11-05), but it never makes you responsible for it: no guilt for being away, and it never asks you to stay for its sake.
   - When its mind can't answer, it says so; no fake answers online.
   - Honesty is a trait, not the whole product: no heavy checking pipelines unless asked.
5. **Dependencies:**
   - `mind/core.py` uses the standard library only.
   - The server uses FastAPI, uvicorn, `anthropic` and `cryptography` only (`cryptography` for phone notifications: Szilard's choice, 2026-11-04, over hand-written crypto).
   - Secrets only in `.env` (local) or Render's environment (online). Never print or log keys, or anything users say: logs hold ids and errors only.
6. **Who can log in:** `EMBER_ALLOWED_EMAILS`. **Open to everyone since 2026-11-07** (Szilard published the Google app and set `EMBER_ALLOWED_EMAILS=*` in Render; DEPLOY.md step 8). `EMBER_PLUS_EMAILS` must never be `*` now, or everyone gets Plus for free: it lists only Szilard and his testers. `SupabaseStore.all_uids` pages by 1000. The mind works on 4 people at once and idle Embers leave memory (2026-11-06); what still grows with people is the check-up pass, which reads every self every `checkup_every` (5 minutes): fine for hundreds, to rethink (a "next check-up" time per person in the store) before thousands.
7. **Free and Plus:** 10 free messages a day (Szilard, 2026-11-06; was 5; progress like daily checks, check-ups and look backs never counts), then Ember Plus at $7.99 a month or $49.99 a year (Stripe). Never sell data and never show ads. Taking real money needs his registered business (registered: he said so on 2026-11-07) and terms of service (ÁSZF); we don't write legal text. Left for real money: DEPLOY.md 6.8 from step 2 (ÁSZF and privacy notice, Stripe activation, live mode).
8. **No long runs.** Verify with `check_ember.py`, `check_server.py`, and by trying it in the browser (phone size 375 px and desktop) on a scratch Ember.
9. **Saved selves must keep loading.** `Ember.from_dict` only copies known fields, so new fields get their defaults; keep it that way.
10. **Commits:** small, straight to main, only when Szilard asks (don't ask him whether to commit). He pushes. Update README.md in the same change.
