**Ember** ("Your companion who checks up on you and grows with you.", Szilard's tagline, 2026-11-06) is a small being with a face, a phone-first web app at **https://yourember.app** (Google login, one Ember per person). It helps you find what to work towards (experiments) and then get there (goals in phases, daily checks, check-ups), and over time it becomes someone who knows your story. Szilard is its first user; he has no project yet, which is exactly the problem it's for.

## Rules (decided; keep them)

1. **Never destroy a life.** Never reset or overwrite `ember_state/` (his old local Ember, kept as an archive), and never touch live data to try things: use a scratch Ember (`--state`) or the checks. Old lives move to `ember_state/archive/`. Online deletion happens only through the user's own "Delete my Ember"; memories are deleted only by Forget.
2. **Memory is perfect for everyone, forever.** It's the core promise and never a paid feature. Keep costs flat by what goes into the prompt, never by forgetting.
3. **Not an imitation animal.** No food, hunger, sleep, seasons or survival world. No simulated time while away: real time passes and it knows how much.
4. **Honest, lightly.**
   - Memories keep the user's exact words and the date; a memory without a matching quote is dropped.
   - Ember is warm and natural ("I missed hearing from you"), and the app doesn't point out what it lacks. Only if asked directly whether it really feels does it answer honestly (software, wants that are real numbers, it doesn't know whether that's feeling). Its mood shows, but it never guilts you for being away or asks you to stay for its sake.
   - When its mind can't answer, it says so: the template mind never answers online in its place.
   - Honesty is a trait, not the product: no heavy checking pipelines unless asked.
5. **Dependencies and secrets.** `mind/core.py` uses the standard library only. The server uses FastAPI, uvicorn, `anthropic` and `cryptography` only (`cryptography` for push: Szilard's choice). Secrets live only in `.env` (local) or Render's environment. Never print or log keys or anything users say: logs hold ids and errors only.
6. **Who can log in:** everyone since 2026-11-07 (`EMBER_ALLOWED_EMAILS=*` in Render). `EMBER_PLUS_EMAILS` must never be `*` (everyone would get Plus free): it lists only Szilard and his testers.
7. **Free and Plus:** 10 free messages a day (talk, suggest, checkin count; daily checks, check-ups, greetings, look backs and month notes never do), then Ember Plus at $7.99 a month or $49.99 a year. Never sell data, never show ads. We don't write legal text.
8. **A deploy never notifies anyone** (2026-11-07). Whatever was handed out is saved with the self, the restart only re-answers your own unanswered lines, and any new rule that makes something due must not fire for everyone at the next restart (old selves start from "now").
9. **Chats never mix** (2026-11-04): a chat's prompt never brings in another chat's words or goals. Anything about all of you (the month note) lives outside the chats.
10. **The model's name is never shown** to users ("an AI language model"; Ember says it's Claude from Anthropic only if asked).
11. **No glass** (2026-11-03): no backdrop blur, gloss or lit edges. Solid surfaces, a warm glow, flat orange buttons. Section labels in sentence case, never all caps.
12. **Don't advertise privacy** (2026-11-07): no "your words are never sold" or "read, download or delete it all" in videos, ads or selling copy; only where needed (privacy, terms, FAQ answers, the app's Download and Delete).
13. **Copy stays true to the app:** price, free limit, 16+, what it really does.
14. **Stripe is shared** with his other products (account "Solid Results"): never change the account's branding, portal, public details or receipts without asking.
15. **Saved selves must keep loading.** `Ember.from_dict` only copies known fields, so new fields get defaults; any backfill is quiet.
16. **No long runs.** Verify with `check_ember.py`, `check_server.py` and the browser (375 px and desktop) on a scratch Ember. Be honest about what only a real iPhone can show.
17. **Commits:** small, straight to main, only when Szilard asks (never ask him whether to commit). Push when he says "commit push". Update README.md and this file in the same change.
18. **Removed on purpose; don't bring back without asking:** traits that grew from how you talk ("Who Ember is becoming", built and removed 2026-11-08); the grid-world Ember (git tag `grid-ember-final`; `git show grid-ember-final:README.md` is its full record); work sessions as a way to start work (old data still loads); weekly / few-times-a-week check-up options.

## Run

```bash
pip install -r requirements.txt       # fastapi, uvicorn, anthropic, cryptography
python ember.py                       # local mode: your Ember in ember_state/, no login; http://localhost:8765
python ember.py --port 8799 --state <scratch dir>   # a separate Ember for trying things (never test on the real one)
python check_ember.py                 # Ember's self: about a second, no LLM
python check_server.py                # the server: fake mind, logins, Stripe, Supabase (on Windows a run sometimes dies
                                      # on a connection reset in the fakes: run it again)
python -m server                      # online mode (Render); needs the env vars in render.yaml
```

## Map

### Server (`server/`)

- `app.py`, `create_app(store, auth, cfg, call, clock, ...)`:
  - `Users` caches one `Ember` per person and saves lines first, then the self. An Ember unused for `IDLE_FOR` (an hour) leaves memory (`evict`, after each check-up pass: saved first, kept if thinking or used meanwhile); background looks (`get(touch=False)`) don't keep it in memory.
  - `/state?v=1` is presence: it calls `arrive`/`update` lazily (no global loop). On a new visit it queues the greeting, or the month note instead (see Becoming someone). `/state?tz=` sets `Ember.tz` (minutes from UTC). `/state?chat=` is that chat's snapshot (`chat`, `chats`, its `talk` and `experiment`, `thinking_in`, `plan`).
  - `MindWorker`: `mind_threads` queues (`EMBER_MIND_THREADS`, default 4); a person always goes to the same one (`queue_of`, crc32), so one person's thoughts stay in order. Token usage is per thread (`llm.take_usage`). Claude calls time out after 30 s with 2 SDK retries, plus one more after `retry_delay`. Still failing: `UNREACHABLE` once and a retry every `retry_every`; non-retryable: `FAILED`; daily cap: `CAPPED`. An unreadable answer is asked once more (`_think`), then `FAILED`. `COUNTED` events spend free messages; `OWN` events (arrive, followup, month) are free and say nothing on failure. Each answer's time is logged (`mind answered <id> (<event>) in Ns`, a warning over 20 s). After a restart, `requeue_unanswered` re-queues only your own unanswered lines (talk, which never pushes).
  - Deleting (2026-11-08, Szilard; "keep the chat" when a goal goes): `/chat/delete {id}` (404 unknown; 409 while Ember is answering in it; `store.delete_lines` first, 503 if that fails; returns the chat to open) and `/goal/delete {id}`. `_think` and `respond` drop an event whose chat or goal is gone (`Ember.gone`), so an answer never lands in another chat.
  - The POST guard: `X-Ember` header and same host (`/stripe/webhook` checks Stripe's signature instead). `/me/export`, `/me/delete` (cancels an active subscription first).
  - Ember Plus: `is_plus` (local mode, `EMBER_PLUS_EMAILS`, or a plan status in `PLUS_STATUSES`); `spend_free` (then 402); `after_reply` (the `LAST_FREE` line marked `paywall`; a check-up reply goes to the phone via `notify`); `/plus/checkout {yearly}`, `/plus/portal`, `/stripe/webhook`. "Manage Ember Plus" only for a running subscription (`plan.manage`); Plus from `EMBER_PLUS_EMAILS` shows "Ember Plus · free for you". Checkout tries the saved customer, then starts fresh by email; Manage with a customer Stripe doesn't know (`resource_missing`) forgets the plan (status `gone`) and opens checkout. Stripe errors are logged with code, field and message; `goPlus` shows what went wrong in a `toast()`.
  - The owner page: `GET /owner/data` (404 unless `EMBER_OWNER_EMAILS` or local; at most once a minute, `OWNER_FOR`). **Counts only**, never words, goal names or memories (`Ember.owner_view`). Shows people, active today/week, new, who comes back, cost (usage × `PRICE_IN`/`PRICE_OUT`), latest feedback.
  - Feedback: `POST /feedback` → `store.add_feedback` (Supabase table `feedback`, or `feedback.jsonl`); keeps email, level, Plus, screen and browser; `FEEDBACK_PER_HOUR` 10; in the export and gone with delete; the log says only who and the kind.
  - Every page sent online (the app's `index()` and `site_page`) gets Cloudflare's visit counter before `</body>` (`counted`).
  - The website: `SITE`/`PAGES`, `<!--#header-->`/`<!--#footer-->` filled from `web/_header.html`/`_footer.html`, `/sitemap.xml`, `/robots.txt`, `web/404.html` for browsers. `index()` marks the online page `<html data-mode="online">`. `/img/` serves images and `.mp4` (`IMAGES`), video in pieces when asked (HTTP Range, 206: iPhones need it).
